Pune's network is genuinely limited compared to Delhi's — many venues on this board (Koregaon Park, Balewadi, Camp's smaller cafés) sit some distance from the nearest station, so this is the closest useful landmark rather than a door-to-door route. Confirm price and timing on the linked page before you travel; small venues change schedules constantly.
